About

Who we are, and what this tool is.

Our mission

Mission statement to come: two or three sentences on cardiovascular health equity for underserved Richmond communities.

The Open Heart Initiative is a student-founded organization bringing free heart health screenings, education, and care navigation to underserved communities in Richmond, VA.

High blood pressure is called "the silent killer": it has no symptoms, and many people are living with it without knowing. We meet people where they already are, in barbershops, churches, and community spaces, check their numbers for free, and help connect anyone at risk to affordable care.

What the risk tool is based on

The published risk framework and guidelines the tool draws on, named plainly, to come.

The tool asks thirteen questions about age, family history, blood pressure, cholesterol, tobacco use, diabetes, activity, diet, weight, current symptoms, and when you last saw a doctor. It adds those into a score out of 25 and sorts that score into lower, moderate, or higher risk. All of that math runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is stored.

Honest scope

The Open Heart Initiative is an early-stage, community-focused effort. Our screening tool is educational and does not replace a medical professional.

Pillar one · Prevention

Find out early, while it is still easy to fix.

Most people with high blood pressure feel completely fine, so the only way to know is to check. We do that two ways: free in-person screenings around Richmond, and the risk check on this page.

The heart risk check

Thirteen questions. About five minutes.

Answer honestly and you get a score out of 25, what is driving it, and one clear next step. No name, no account, no cost.

Free screenings in the neighborhood

We bring blood pressure checks to the places people already go: barbershops, churches, and community spaces, at the same spot every month. We also loan home blood pressure cuffs to people who need to track their numbers between visits.

What the tool collects

Full data note to come.

What we can say today: your answers are scored in your own browser and are never sent to us or to anyone else. The one exception is your ZIP code, and only if you choose to enter it. It goes to our clinic-lookup service to find nearby health centers, and it is not stored after that. You can skip the ZIP step and still see your full results.

If it is an emergency

Chest pain, pressure or tightness, sudden shortness of breath, or a blood pressure reading of 180/120 or higher: do not wait on a website. Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. ERs must treat you in an emergency, insured or not.

Pillar four · Recovery

Nobody should manage this alone.

A diagnosis or a heart event is the start of something, not the end of it. This pillar is the one we are still building, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise.

What this will cover

  • Understanding your medications, and what to do about side effects
  • Warning signs worth acting on, and which ones can wait

  • Free re-checks, so your progress is tracked over time
